## Organization Structure

Breakout is a student-led innovation and entrepreneurship organization based at PUCP (Pontificia Universidad Catolica del Peru). The workspace is owned/managed by Andrea Melo. The organization is structured into three main areas:

1. **Community & Experience** — Events, community engagement, UX, culture
2. **Growth & Innovation** — Growth strategy, content, new initiatives
3. **Partnerships & Programs** — Alliances, partnerships, programs, revenue generation

The central hub is the **Breakout Wiki** page, which contains a database and links to all areas. Below it sit the area pages, event pages, member databases, calendars, and task boards.
