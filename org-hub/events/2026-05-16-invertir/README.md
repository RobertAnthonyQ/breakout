## Event Description

> Una conversación sobre la realidad de invertir en el Perú: cómo el ecosistema de inversión está cambiando, qué oportunidades existen para estudiantes, y aprendizajes directos de quienes construyen las plataformas que democratizan el acceso a la bolsa en la región.

This was Breakout's third documented event and its first investing-themed one — fitting under the **Breakout Capital** pillar of the 2026 plan (founder funnel + ecosystem articulation).

## Speakers

### Diego Torres — Co-founder & CEO, Trii

- **Org:** [Trii](https://trii.co/) — Colombian wealthtech / investment app
- **Background:**
  - Co-founded Trii in 2019 (with Esteban Peñaloza and Carlos Guayara)
  - Trii is YC-backed; investors and partners include **Grupo Bancolombia, Y Combinator, and Harvard University**
  - Operates in **Colombia, Perú and Chile**; ~500K users and ~US$300M in assets under administration
  - Responsible for **~95% of all new investment accounts opened in Perú in 2024**
  - Processes ~55% of transactions on the Colombian Stock Exchange (BVC)
- **Relevance:** Directly embodies the event premise — a founder who built the rails for retail investing in Perú and the region.
