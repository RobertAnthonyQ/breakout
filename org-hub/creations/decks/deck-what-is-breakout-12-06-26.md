## 01

# What is Breakout?

---

## The community

Breakout is PUCP's **student innovation and entrepreneurship community**.

- 15 active members · 3 areas
- Born out of the **University Innovation Fellows** program (Stanford d.school + University of Twente)
- Current semester: **26-1**

> **Surround yourself with people who have the same mindset.** Events, alliances and capital — everything the traditional university path doesn't give you.

---

## Why we exist

<div class="columns">
<div>

## ZERO ONE — Building a Startup

Panel with founders **building right now**, not recounting a finished success.

- 6 founders from 3 countries: <span class="electric">Peru, Bolivia, Chile</span>
- Sectors: proptech, agritech, healthtech/AI, gig economy, mobility
- Speakers from Dommies, Agroglim, XRAI, Favorcito, Indies, Rodar
- Format: panel + Menti Q&A + post-event interviews

> Bust early-stage myths · Motivate people to launch their idea · Spark the fire.

---

We co-organized the **8th track (Business & Venture)** of AXIS with Lead PUCP.

<div class="columns">
<div>

### Block 1 — Global Opportunities
Okaren Cabrera (Harvard Aspire, Beca 18) and Arianna Yauri (DAAD, Huawei) — how to access the global ecosystem from Peru.

</div>
<div>

### Block 2 — Build with AI
Freddy led a hands-on workshop: **a working prototype in 90 minutes with no coding.**

</div>

> Mass outreach to **~21,000 students** across 7 PUCP faculties.

## INVERTIR — the reality in Peru

First event under the **Breakout Capital** pillar.

- **Diego Torres**, co-founder & CEO of **Trii** (YC, Bancolombia, Harvard)
- Trii: ~500K users, ~US$300M in assets, ~95% of new investment accounts in Peru in 2024
- **6 Harvard Business School students** as guests
- **176 sign-ups** in 3 days · 109 the day before (peak)
- Audience: 72% PUCP · 57% in the 23–27 age range
