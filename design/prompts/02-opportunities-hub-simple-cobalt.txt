1. DESIGN DIRECTION & SIMPLIFICATION GOALS
================================================================================
Feedback from Freddy:
1. "Make it more simple": Eliminate visual noise, reduce nested rails, remove decorative sidebars, and focus on one clear, fast, frictionless discovery flow.
2. "Add more of the brand blue": Cobalt #214FDD is the core of Breakout's identity (per official brandbook: "The brand. Hero backgrounds, cover slides, primary buttons...").

We adopt a bold, immersive Cobalt Blue (#214FDD) Hero Header that immediately anchors the brand, followed by a clean, spacious, modern card catalog on light ground (#F8FAFC).

Color Palette:
- Brand Cobalt (Hero ground, primary accents): #214FDD
- Dark Cobalt (Top navigation bar ground): #183EB5
- Spark Cyan (Accent tags & highlights on blue): #6CE5E8
- White: #FFFFFF
- Light Ground (Body section): #F8FAFC
- Card Surface: #FFFFFF
- Card Borders: #E2E8F0 (1px solid)
- Text on Cobalt: #FFFFFF (Primary), #D7DFF9 (Secondary / muted), #6CE5E8 (Cyan accent)
- Text on Light Ground: #0A0A0A (Headings), #475569 (Body), #64748B (Muted)
- Status / Tag colors:
  - Grant / Subvención: #059669 (Text), #ECFDF5 (Bg), #A7F3D0 (Border)
  - Aceleradora: #214FDD (Text), #EFF6FF (Bg), #BFDBFE (Border)
  - Hackathon: #7C3AED (Text), #F5F3FF (Bg), #DDD6FE (Border)
  - Beca: #D97706 (Text), #FFFBEB (Bg), #FDE68A (Border)

Typography:
- System Font: Inter / Inter Tight
- High contrast, clean whitespace, zero clutter.
