# Breakout Brand Styling

## Overview

This skill applies Breakout's official brand identity to any artifact (presentation, document, HTML, poster, social post, etc.).

**Full brand reference:** Always read `.agents/breakout-brand.md` before applying brand styles.

**Keywords**: branding, identidad visual, brand colors, tipografía, Breakout brand, visual formatting, diseño

---

## Brand Summary

### Colors

| Rol | Nombre | HEX |
|-----|--------|-----|
| **Primario / identitario** | Azul Breakout | `#2430FF` |
| Fondo neutro cálido | Gris cálido | `#F1EAE4` |
| Fondo neutro frío | Gris frío | `#E1E1E1` |
| Fondo oscuro (bold) | Negro | `#0A0A0A` |
| Texto | Gris oscuro | `#1A1A1A` |

**Rules:**
- `#2430FF` is the identity color — use for emphasis, CTAs, high-impact backgrounds, typographic highlights
- Never use pure black (`#000000`) for text — always use dark grays
- Never mix `#2430FF` with other blues

### Typography

| Use | Font | Fallback |
|-----|------|----------|
| Headings / titles | **Poppins Bold/SemiBold** | Arial Black |
| Body text | Raleway | Calibri |
| Italic emphasis | *Raleway Italic* | — |
| Blue highlight | **Raleway Bold** in `#2430FF` | — |
| Logo wordmark | EquitanSans-Bold | Poppins |

**Rules:**
- Never more than 2 different fonts in a single piece
- "Breakout" wordmark always in EquitanSans or Poppins — never omit from brand pieces

### Visual Style — Two Registers

**Register 1 — Bold / Impact** (large events):
- Background: solid blue `#2430FF` or black
- Giant titles filling most of the piece (white or high-contrast)
- Decorative elements: geometric lines, dot networks, wireframe figures
- Filled icons only

**Register 2 — Minimal Editorial** (organic content / announcements):
- Background: warm gray `#F1EAE4` or off-white
- Poppins in `#2430FF` for main text; dark gray for secondary
- Lots of breathing room — don't fill the space

### UI Elements
- Buttons: pill-shaped (very rounded corners)
- Primary: solid `#2430FF` + white text
- Secondary: outline in `#2430FF`
- Tertiary: warm gray `#F1EAE4`
- Icons: always filled, never outline

---

## How to Apply

When applying Breakout's brand to any artifact:

1. Read `.agents/breakout-brand.md` for the full brand guide
2. Use `#2430FF` as the dominant accent or background
3. Use Poppins for headings, Raleway for body
4. Never use pure black text
5. Keep "Breakout" wordmark visible in the piece
6. Use filled icons only
7. Buttons always rounded (pill shape)
